
# Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions
1. Robot has two arms, two legs, two hands, and two feet, with a head that has eyes. All of these can move in any direction, so long as they are attached to the robot. Robot's eyes can see. There is a loaf of bread on the floor next to a table with a peanut butter jar, jelly jar, and a butter knife ahead.
2. Robot will move one leg forward, making sure its foot is on the ground. 
3. Robot will take move the other leg forward, placing its foot on the ground.
4. Robot will stop.
5. Robot will move its head, and will stop once it sees a loaf of bread on the ground. 
6. Robot will move both hands to pick up loaf of bread. 
7. Robot will take the loaf, and move its hand to place it on top of the table.
8. Robot will open plastic covering of loaf, and take out two slices with hand.
9. Robot will place slices on table with hand
10. Robot will take butter knife with hand
11. Robot will move its hand to put butter knife in jelly jar
12. Robot will move hand to take jelly out of jelly jar with butter knife
13. Robot will move arm towards one slice of bread
14. Robot will place jelly covered knife onto bread
15. Robot will move hand to put jelly on bread
16. Robot will stop
17. Robot will move its hand to put butter knife in peanut butter jar
18. Robot will move hand to take peanut butter out of peanut butter jar with butter knife
19. Robot will move arm towards untouched slice of bread
20. Robot will place peanut butter covered knife onto bread
21. Robot will move hand to put peanut butter on bread
22. Robot will stop
23. Robot will move hand to grab jelly bread
24. Robot will move free hand to grab peanut butter bread
25. Robot will move both hands towards each other
26. Robot will push both breads together, creating a sandwich

## Done When
- Robot is done when it moves hand to table, and places the newly made sandwich flat on one side on the table
